## 🌟 Executive Mission & About Me

I am an **AI Systems Developer & Software Architect** from **Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, India**. Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) at **ITM GIDA Gorakhpur**, I engineer production-grade civic intelligence platforms, automated computer vision verification gates, and zero-brokerage/zero-cost cloud microservices that solve real-world community challenges.

- 🌿 **Lead Architect & Developer of CleanConnect AI (EcoTrack):** Next-Gen AI Civic Cleanliness & Municipal Waste Intelligence platform (compliant with IEEE Std 830-1998) featuring Google Gemini Vision multimodal hazard triage, OpenCV SSIM 75%+ before-and-after proof verification gate, asynchronous FastAPI microservices, and zero-cost ($0 USD) cloud infrastructure across Vercel & Render.
- ⚡ **Architect of Smart Campus Energy:** Real-time IoT power telemetry with heuristic peak-load anomaly detection and automated energy conservation workflows.
- 💻 **Founder & Builder of Personal Developer Vault:** Cyberpunk dark-glassmorphism portfolio ecosystem with 1-click printable ATS-friendly resume engine and smart client-side device routing.
- 🏅 **Honors & Credentials:** Certified in Advance Diploma in Computer Applications (**ADCA** with distinction from MDCIT, 2024), Basic Computer Certification (NIIT Foundation, 2023), and active builder at ITM GIDA Gorakhpur.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Multimodal AI (Google Gemini Vision), Python FastAPI, OpenCV / SSIM verification engines, asynchronous microservices, SQLite WAL, and zero-cost cloud deployment architectures.
